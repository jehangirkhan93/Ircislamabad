Spirometry (Phephron Ka Test) is the gold-standard breathing test to measure how fast and how much air you can blow out. At IRC G-11 Markaz, Islamabad, spirometry is performed with clinical interpretation by Dr. Jehangir Khan & Dr. Muhammad Israr.

Spirometry Procedure - What Happens?

  1. You sit straight, nose clip applied, take deep breath in
  2. Blow out HARD and FAST into spirometer mouthpiece for at least 6 seconds
  3. Repeat 3 times to get best effort (ATS/ERS criteria)
  4. Bronchodilator (salbutamol) may be given and test repeated after 15 minutes to check reversibility
  5. Report generated same day with FEV1, FVC, FEV1/FVC

Preparation for Spirometry

  • Avoid smoking 1 hour before, vigorous exercise 30 min before, heavy meal 2 hours before
  • Avoid alcohol 4 hours before, tight clothing that restricts breathing
  • Continue medicines unless clinic tells you to hold — bring list of inhalers
  • Bring previous spirometry, chest X-ray, asthma diary if any
  • For reversibility test: Avoid short-acting inhaler (salbutamol) 4-6 hours if possible, long-acting 12-24 hours — only if doctor advised

Who Needs Spirometry?

  • Chronic cough, wheeze, shortness of breath — Asthma assessment
  • Smoker with breathlessness — COPD assessment
  • Follow-up after treatment to check improvement
  • Pre-employment / fitness / allergy related breathlessness
  • Unexplained chest tightness

Spirometry vs Full PFTs - Difference

Spirometry = 1 Test → Airflow (FEV1, FVC)
PFTs = 5 Tests Panel → Spirometry + FeNO (inflammation) + Lung Age + CO Monitor + Peak Flow Diary

Normal FEV1 FVC kya hai?

Predicted ka 80% se zyada normal. Ratio >0.70 normal. Exact interpretation age/height/gender ke hisab se doctor karega.
