Spirometry Kya Hai? Phephron Ka Test

In this test, you blow air forcefully into a machine. Machine measures FVC (how much air your lungs can hold) and FEV1 (how fast you can blow out in 1 sec). Ratio FEV1/FVC tells if you have Asthma, COPD, or lung blockage. Graph print with normal values provided.

When Do You Need Spirometry?

  • Cough more than 8 weeks, not responding to medicines
  • Shortness of breath on walking or stairs
  • History of smoking - 10+ years bidi/cigarette
  • Diagnosed asthma but want to check control - is inhaler working?
  • Wheezing sound in chest, chest tightness
  • Before any major surgery - fitness for anesthesia
  • Exposure to dust, chemical factory, stone cutting

Spirometry vs FeNO vs X-ray - Difference?

X-ray shows structure, Spirometry shows function, FeNO shows allergic inflammation. For asthma, we need all 3: X-ray to rule out TB, Spirometry to confirm blockage, FeNO to confirm allergy. At IRC we do all in one visit.
