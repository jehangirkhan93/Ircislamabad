Spirometry Kya Hai?

In this test, you blow air forcefully into a machine. Machine measures how much air your lungs can hold and how fast you can blow out. It tells if you have Asthma, COPD, or any other lung blockage.

When Do You Need Spirometry?

  • If you have cough more than 8 weeks
  • Shortness of breath
  • History of smoking
  • Diagnosed asthma but want to check control
  • Before any major surgery
