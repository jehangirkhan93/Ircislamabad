1. TB Kya Hai? Pakistan Me Situation

Tuberculosis (TB) ek bacterial infection hai jo Mycobacterium tuberculosis se hota hai. Pakistan WHO high-burden countries me 5th number par hai. Rozana hazaron naye cases diagnose hote hain. TB sirf gareebon ki bemari nahi — achi ghiza walon ko bhi ho sakti hai agar immunity kam ho ya close contact ho.

TB ka bacteria hawa me khansi / cheenk se phailta hai. Ek TB patient bina ilaj ke saal me 10-15 logon ko infect kar sakta hai. Is liye early diagnosis aur DOTS poora karna bohat zaroori hai.

Islamabad me IRC G-11 Markaz chest specialist clinic hai jahan spirometry, allergy, bronchoscopy aur GeneXpert ki sahulat ek jagah par hai — TB aur asthma me farq karna aasan ho jata hai.

3. TB Ki Alamat (Symptoms) Detail Se

Pulmonary TB Ki Common Alamat:

  • Khansi >2 haftay: Pehle khushk, phir balgham wali. Ilaaj se theek na hona. Asthma me khansi raat ko zyada, TB me poora din.
  • Sham ko halka bukhar (99-101°F): Subah theek, sham ko bukhar, thakan. Yeh TB ki khas alamat hai.
  • Wazan kam hona aur bhok khatam: 1 mahine me 3-5 kg wazan kam, kapray dheelay.
  • Raat ko pasina: Neend me itna pasina ke kapray badalne parain.
  • Balgham me khoon (Hemoptysis): Khoon ki chittein ya khoon ki khansi — foran doctor ko dikhayein.
  • Seene me dard, saans phoolna: Agar pleural effusion ya cavity ho to.

Extrapulmonary TB Ki Alamat:

  • Gardan par dard-less ghilti jo barhti jaye (Lymph Node TB)
  • Kamar dard jo theek na ho, bukhar ke saath (Spine TB)
  • Pait me dard, wazan kam, bukhar (Abdominal TB)
  • Jorhon me soojan jo antibiotics se theek na ho

4. Kis Ko TB Ka Khatra Zyada Hai?

  • TB patient ke saath rehne walay ghar walay (close contact)
  • Diabetes, smoking, COPD, asthma par steroid use
  • HIV, chemotherapy, dialysis patients (low immunity)
  • Bachay aur buzurg, ghiza ki kami (malnutrition)
  • Smoking aur ghutka / naswar — lungs kamzor
  • Doctors, nurses, lab workers

5. TB Tests in Islamabad - GeneXpert, X-ray, Sputum Culture

IRC G-11 me complete TB workup 1 din me:

Step-wise Testing at IRC:

Step 1 - Digital Chest X-ray (PA + Lateral): First screening. Infiltration, cavity, pleural effusion dekhta hai. 10 min me report.

Step 2 - Sputum GeneXpert MTB/RIF (Gold Standard): WHO recommended. Subah ka gehra balgham ka sample — 2 ghante me result + Rifampicin resistance (MDR) batata hai. Sensitivity 95% pulmonary TB me.

Step 3 - Sputum AFB Smear & Culture: Agar GeneXpert negative lekin shak zyada ho to culture gold standard hai — 2-6 haftay me result. Drug-susceptibility testing (DST) is se hota hai.

Step 4 - Bronchoscopy with BAL GeneXpert (IRC me available): Jab balgham na aa raha ho, X-ray doubtful ho, ya tumor vs TB ka farq karna ho. Bronchoscopy se andar se wash le kar GeneXpert karte hain — accuracy 85%. General anesthesia nahi, local spray.

Step 5 - Pleural Fluid / Lymph Node FNAC: Pleural TB ke liye fluid ADA, protein, GeneXpert. Gardan ki ghilti ke liye FNAC + GeneXpert.

Step 6 - Blood Tests: CBC, ESR, CRP, LFT, KFT, RBS, HIV, HbA1c before treatment — dawa liver par asar karti hai to baseline zaroori.

Tip: Subah uthte hi pehla gehra balgham ka sample sab se behtar. Mouth wash karke, gehra saans le kar balgham nikalein, thook nahi. 2 samples ideal — one spot (clinic me), one early morning (ghar se).

7. MDR & XDR TB - Resistant TB Ka Ilaj

MDR-TB (Multi-Drug Resistant): Rifampicin + Isoniazid dono se resistant. Wajah: adhi dawai chorna, dose miss karna, galat regimen.

  • GeneXpert RIF resistance batata hai → LPA (Line Probe Assay) + Culture DST se confirm
  • Ilaj 9-12 months, second-line drugs (Bedaquiline, Linezolid, Moxifloxacin, Clofazimine) — mehenga aur side effects zyada
  • Cure rate 60-70%, regular follow-up zaroori

XDR-TB: MDR + Fluoroquinolone + second-line injectable se bhi resistant — aur mushkil ilaj.

Prevention: Pehli dafa hi DOTS poora karein, koi dawai miss na karein, doctor ke mashwaray ke bagair dawai na chorhein.

8. Dawaon Ke Side Effects & Liver Test

  • Peshab narangi (orange): Rifampicin se — normal hai
  • Matli, bhok kam: Pyrazinamide se — khane ke baad lein, doctor ko batayein
  • Jorhon me dard: Pyrazinamide se uric acid barhta hai — pani zyada piyein
  • Nazar dhundli: Ethambutol se — agar nazar kam lage to foran doctor ko batayein, color vision test karayein
  • Jaundice (peeli rangat): Isoniazid, Rifampicin, Pyrazinamide liver par asar kar sakti hain — agar aankhein peeli, bhok bilkul khatam, ulti aaye to foran LFT karayein aur doctor ko dikhayein. Alcohol bilkul mana.

IRC me har month LFT, wazan, symptoms check karte hain.

9. TB Me Diet & Nutrition Plan

High Protein + High Calorie Diet — Wazan kam na hone dein:

  • Anda roz 1-2, chicken, fish, daal, lobia — protein lungs repair ke liye
  • Doodh, dahi, lassi — calcium aur protein
  • Roti, chawal, aalu — energy ke liye
  • Mausami phal: kela, amrood, orange (Vitamin C)
  • Sabzian: palak, gajar, bhindi — vitamins
  • Pani 8-10 glass, ORS agar paseena zyada

Avoid: Smoking, naswar, ghutka, sharab (liver damage), bahar ka khana, zyada mirch masala, cold drinks. Chaye kam piyein.

Wazan har haftay check karein — agar barh raha hai to ilaj kaam kar raha hai.

10. Kya TB Contagious Hai? Ghar Me Ehtiyat

  • Pulmonary TB khansi / cheenk se hawa me phailta hai — extrapulmonary (gland, haddi) contagious nahi
  • Ilaj shuru hone ke 2-3 haftay baad 90% contagiousness khatam — tab tak mask pehne, alag kamre me soyein, kamra hawa-daar, dhoop aaye
  • Balgham tissue me le kar band dustbin me phenke, farsh par na thookna
  • Bachon ka BCG vaccine zaroor lagwaye — TB se bachata hai
  • Family screening: Ghar ke sab logon ka X-ray aur symptoms check — khas tor par bachay, buzurg, diabetes wale
  • Bartan, kapray alag karne ki zaroorat nahi — sirf hawa se phailta hai, chhoone se nahi

12. FAQs - TB in Islamabad

TB ki alamat kya hain?

2 haftay se zyada khansi, sham ko halka bukhar, wazan kam, bhok khatam, raat ko pasina, balgham me khoon TB ki alamat hain.

GeneXpert test behtar hai ya sputum culture?

GeneXpert 2 ghante me result + MDR batata hai — screening ke liye behtar. Culture confirmatory hai jab GeneXpert negative ho. Dono IRC par available.

TB ka ilaj kitne arse tak?

Normal TB 6 months, MDR 9-12 months. Dawa rozana, bina nagha. Poora course zaroori.

Kya TB ka test bronchoscopy se hota hai?

Agar balgham na aaye to bronchoscopy BAL GeneXpert karte hain. IRC G-11 me facility hai. Bronchoscopy cost alag hai.

TB contagious kab tak rehta hai?

Ilaj ke 2-3 haftay baad contagiousness 90% khatam. Tab tak mask, hawa-daar kamra, family screening.

TB me diet kaisi ho?

High protein — anda, chicken, daal, doodh, phal. Wazan barhana zaroori. Smoking, sharab mana.

TB aur asthma me farq?

Asthma me inhaler se aram, TB me infection. Spirometry asthma me, GeneXpert TB me. Dono me khansi lekin TB me sham ko bukhar aur wazan kam zyada.

TB treatment cost?

Bachay ko TB se kaise bachayein?

BCG vaccine paidaish par, ghar me TB patient ho to bachay ka X-ray + test, ghiza achi, hawa-daar ghar.
