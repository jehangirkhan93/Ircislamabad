TB (Tuberculosis) - Symptoms, Test and DOTS Treatment in Islamabad

By Dr. Jehangir Khan, Consultant Pulmonologist | Reviewed by Dr. Muhammad Israr | IRC Islamabad, G-11 Markaz | Updated 28 August 2026 | Medically reviewed content

Tuberculosis (TB) is still very common in Pakistan. Islamabad Respiratory Clinic (IRC) in G-11 Markaz is a specialized center for TB diagnosis with GeneXpert, Digital X-ray, Sputum Culture and DOTS treatment under supervision of Pulmonologist in Islamabad.

Early diagnosis saves lungs and prevents spread to family. If you have cough more than 2 weeks with evening fever, get tested early.

TB Ki Alamat Kya Hain? (TB Symptoms)

  • 2 haftay se zyada khansi (Cough more than 2 weeks) - sab se common alamat
  • Sham ko bukhar (Evening low-grade fever 99-100°F)
  • Wazan ka kam hona (Unexplained weight loss, bhok kam lagna)
  • Raaton ko pasina ana (Night sweats - kapray geele ho jana)
  • Balagham me khoon ana (Hemoptysis)
  • Seene me dard, thakan, kamzori

Note: TB sirf lungs me nahi, lymph nodes, haddi aur pait me bhi ho sakti hai. Har jagah ki alamat mukhtalif hai.

TB Test in Islamabad - GeneXpert, X-ray, Sputum Culture

At IRC G-11 we offer complete workup:

  • Digital Chest X-ray: First screening - shows infiltration, cavity
  • Sputum GeneXpert MTB/RIF: Gold standard, WHO recommended. Result in 2 hours, also tells Rifampicin resistance (MDR-TB)
  • Sputum AFB Culture: Confirms diagnosis when GeneXpert negative
  • Bronchoscopy with BAL GeneXpert: When sputum not coming or X-ray doubtful - we have bronchoscopy facility
  • Blood tests - CBC, ESR, HIV screening before treatment

Tip: Subah ke balgham ka sample sab se behtar hota hai. 2 samples - one spot, one morning.

TB Ka Ilaj Kitna Arsa Chalta Hai? DOTS Treatment

Drug-sensitive TB: 6 months (2 months intensive 4 drugs + 4 months 2 drugs). Drug-resistant TB (MDR): 9-12 months with second-line drugs.

At IRC, we follow National TB Program (NTP) DOTS protocol. It is very important to complete full course, otherwise TB becomes resistant and spreads. We provide free counseling, monthly weight, liver function check and follow-up for full 6 months.

Is TB Contagious? Ehtiyat Kya Karni Chahiye?

Yes, pulmonary TB spreads through air when patient coughs/sneezes. Precautions:

  • Patient should wear surgical mask for first 2-3 weeks of treatment
  • Keep room ventilated, sunlight ana chahiye
  • Family members should get screened - chest X-ray and symptoms check
  • Bachon ko BCG vaccine lagwana zaroori hai
  • Balgham ko covered dustbin me phenkna, farsh par na thookna
