What is Pleuroscopy?

Pleuroscopy is a day-care, minimally invasive procedure where a small HD camera is inserted into the pleural space (between lung and chest wall) to directly see the cause of water around lungs, pleural thickening, or recurrent effusion. It has 95% diagnostic accuracy vs 40% for fluid tests alone.

Who Needs It?

  • Recurrent pleural effusion with unknown cause
  • Exudative effusion where fluid cytology is negative
  • Suspected Pleural TB or Malignancy / Mesothelioma
  • When closed pleural biopsy has failed
  • Pleural thickening on CT

How it's Done at IRC (30-45 min)

  1. Chest ultrasound to mark safe entry point - no blind puncture
  2. Local anesthesia + mild sedation - you are awake, no pain
  3. 1 small cut (< 1 cm) - HD camera inserted
  4. Pleura inspected on monitor - targeted biopsies taken
  5. Fluid drained, pleurodesis if needed for recurrence prevention
  6. 2-4 hours observation & same-day discharge
