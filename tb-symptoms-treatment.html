TB (Tuberculosis) - Symptoms, Test and Treatment in Islamabad

By Dr. Jehangir Khan, Pulmonologist | IRC Islamabad, G-11 Markaz | Updated August 2026

ADVERTISEMENT

Tuberculosis (TB) is still very common in Pakistan. Islamabad Respiratory Clinic (IRC) is a specialized center for TB diagnosis and DOTS treatment in G-11 Markaz.

TB Ki Alamat Kya Hain?

  • 2 haftay se zyada khansi (Cough more than 2 weeks)
  • Sham ko bukhar (Evening fever)
  • Wazan ka kam hona (Weight loss)
  • Raaton ko pasina ana (Night sweats)
  • Balagham me khoon ana

TB Test in Islamabad

At IRC we do: Digital Chest X-ray, Sputum GeneXpert test (most accurate TB test), Sputum culture, and Bronchoscopy if needed. GeneXpert test result comes in 2 hours and also tells if TB is drug-resistant.

TB Ka Ilaj Kitna Arsa Chalta Hai?

Normal TB treatment is 6 months. Drug-resistant TB treatment is 9-12 months. It is very important to complete full course, otherwise TB becomes resistant. At IRC, we provide free counseling and follow-up for full 6 months.

Is TB Contagious?

Yes, TB spreads through air when patient coughs. Patient should wear mask for first 2 weeks of treatment and keep room ventilated. Family members should also get screened.
