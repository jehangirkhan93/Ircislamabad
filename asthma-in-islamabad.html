Asthma in Islamabad: Why It's Rising, and How We Diagnose It

By Islamabad Respiratory Clinic (IRC), G-11 Markaz, Islamabad · Medically reviewed by Dr Muhammad Israr & Dr Jehangir Khan

If you find yourself wheezing during the winter smog, waking up short of breath after Islamabad's spring pollen season, or reaching for an inhaler every time the weather turns, you are not alone. Asthma cases in Islamabad have been climbing steadily over the past decade.

Why Is Asthma So Common in Islamabad?

1. Seasonal Smog and Vehicular Pollution

Every autumn and winter, Islamabad and the wider Rawalpindi-Islamabad belt experience thick smog caused by vehicle emissions, dust, and crop residue burning.

2. Paper Mulberry and Pollen Allergy

Islamabad has one of the highest concentrations of Paper Mulberry trees. Every spring, March-April, these trees release enormous quantities of airborne pollen.

3. Construction and Road Dust

Constant expansion generates fine dust that worsens airway inflammation.

4. Biomass and Waste Burning

Open burning of garden waste, garbage, and biomass fuel contains irritant gases that trigger asthma.

5. Sudden Weather Changes

Rapid temperature and humidity swings trigger airway hyper-reactivity.

6. Indoor Allergens

Dust mites, mould, and pet dander add indoor triggers.
