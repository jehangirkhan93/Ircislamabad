Bronchoscopy in Islamabad - When and Why is it Done?

By Dr. Jehangir Khan, Pulmonologist | IRC Islamabad, G-11 Markaz | Updated August 2026

ADVERTISEMENT

Bronchoscopy is a procedure where a thin flexible camera is passed through nose into lungs to see inside airways directly. At IRC Islamabad, Dr. Jehangir Khan does diagnostic bronchoscopy safely.

When is Bronchoscopy Needed?

  • Blood in sputum (Balagham me khoon)
  • Non-resolving pneumonia (X-ray not clearing)
  • Suspected lung cancer or TB
  • Foreign body inhalation
  • To take biopsy or sample (BAL)

Is Bronchoscopy Painful?

No, it is done under local anesthesia spray and mild sedation. Patient is awake but comfortable. Most patients say it was much easier than they thought. Procedure takes 10-15 minutes.

Bronchoscopy Cost and Facility at IRC

At IRC G-11 Markaz, bronchoscopy is done in sterile environment with full monitoring. Cost includes doctor fee, medicines, and biopsy charges. Much lower than private hospitals in Islamabad. We also provide video recording of procedure to patient if needed.

Precautions

Do not eat 6 hours before procedure. Bring attendant. After procedure, do not eat for 2 hours until throat numbness goes.
