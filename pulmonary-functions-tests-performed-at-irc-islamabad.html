Why Islamabad Respiratory Clinic Is a Leading Choice for Asthma Management in Islamabad

Asthma Ka Mukammal Ilaj - 5 Point Check at IRC G-11

70% asthma patients in Islamabad remain uncontrolled because they get only inhaler prescription without testing. At IRC we do complete asthma check:

  • 1. Spirometry PFT: Confirms asthma and severity (FEV1/FVC ratio). Best American spirometer with graphs.
  • 2. FeNO Test: Decides if steroids will help or not. If FeNO >50 ppb, inhaled steroids will strongly help. Saves unnecessary steroid tablets.
  • 3. Allergy Skin Prick Test: Finds root cause - dust mite, pollen, mold, cat. Without this, asthma never fully controls in Islamabad.
  • 4. Inhaler Technique Training: 90% patients use inhaler wrong. We check technique with placebo devices, spacer training.
  • 5. Asthma Action Plan + Peak Flow Diary: Written plan for emergency, when to increase inhaler, when to come to ER.

Result: Patients who complete 5-point check at IRC have 80% fewer emergency visits. We also offer allergy vaccine immunotherapy for permanent cure and biologic selection (Dupilumab, Omalizumab, Mepolizumab) for severe persistent asthma based on FeNO + IgE + Eosinophils.

FeNO Test Details - Why Steroids Will Help or Not

FeNO ye batata hai ki aap ki jo bemari hai usme steroid ka role hai ya nahi hai. Ye aap ki tashkhees me help karti hai ke dama hai ya nahi aur steroid ki zaroorat hai ya nahi.

FeNO ValueMeaningAction at IRC
<25 ppbLow - No allergic inflammationSteroids may NOT help - Check GERD, sinus
25-50 ppbIntermediateLow dose ICS inhaler trial
>50 ppbHigh - Eosinophilic asthmaSteroids WILL help + consider biologic

FeNO for Biologic Selection - Severe Persistent Asthma

  • FeNO high + IgE high → Omalizumab (Anti-IgE)
  • FeNO high + Eosinophils high → Dupilumab (Anti-IL4/13) or Mepolizumab/Benralizumab (Anti-IL5)
  • At IRC we do FeNO + CBC + IgE + Allergy test to select right biologic, avoids trial and error.
