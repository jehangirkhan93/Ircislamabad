More than 70% of asthma patients in Islamabad have dust mite allergy. Without allergy testing, asthma never fully controls. IRC G-11 Markaz has advanced allergy testing facility.

What is Skin Prick Allergy Test?

We put small drops of common allergens (dust, pollen, cat, fungus) on your forearm and do a tiny prick. After 15 minutes, we see which allergen makes red bump. It tells exactly what you are allergic to.

Common Allergies in Islamabad

  • Dust mite (Ghar ki dhool) - most common
  • Pollen allergy (Spring season March-April)
  • Mold / fungus (Seelan wali dewar)
  • Cat/dog dander
  • Food allergy causing nasal symptoms

Treatment After Allergy Test

Based on result, Dr. Jehangir Khan gives 3-step plan: 1) Avoidance measures, 2) Anti-allergy medicines and nasal spray, 3) Allergy immunotherapy (allergy vaccine) for permanent cure - available at IRC.

Allergy Vaccine (Immunotherapy)

If you have severe dust allergy for years, allergy vaccine can cure it permanently in 3 years. It is like corona vaccine - small doses of allergen given to make your body tolerant. Success rate is 80%.
