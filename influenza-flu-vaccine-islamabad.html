Influenza (Flu) Vaccine: Purpose, Dose, Safety and Follow-up

By Dr Jehangir Khan, Consultant Pulmonologist · Reviewed by Dr Muhammad Israr · Updated 4 Oct 2026

Key points this season
  • Influvac is in short supply this season.
  • At IRC we advise Vaxigrip or Anflu, whichever is available.
  • For maximum protection in high-risk patients, Dr Jehangir Khan advises two doses of Anflu, one month apart.
  • The goal of vaccination is to prevent flu complications, above all pneumonia, and to reduce deaths.

Why flu vaccination matters

Flu is not just a bad cold. The influenza virus infects the airways and can cause viral pneumonia, or weaken the lungs so that bacteria cause a second pneumonia. It can also trigger asthma and COPD attacks, worsen heart failure and diabetes, and raise the risk of heart attack in the weeks after infection. In Pakistan, flu is most common in the winter months. The vaccine does not always stop infection, but vaccinated people who do catch flu are less likely to develop pneumonia, need hospital care or die.

Purpose of the vaccine

  • Prevent influenza illness, or make it milder and shorter
  • Prevent pneumonia and other complications
  • Reduce hospital admissions and deaths, especially in the elderly and people with chronic disease
  • Prevent asthma and COPD flare-ups caused by flu
  • Protect people around you, such as babies and elderly relatives

Who should get it? (Indications)

  • Adults aged 65 and over
  • Children aged 6 months to 5 years
  • Pregnant women, in any trimester
  • Chronic lung disease: asthma, COPD, bronchiectasis, past TB damage
  • Heart disease, diabetes, kidney or liver disease, severe obesity
  • Anyone with a lingering cough or breathlessness: check your lungs first with lung function tests
  • Weak immunity: HIV, long-term steroids, chemotherapy, transplant
  • Healthcare workers, carers of high-risk people, and Hajj or Umrah pilgrims
  • Anyone who wants to lower their risk of flu

Dose, route and schedule

ItemDetail
TypeInactivated (killed) vaccine. It cannot give you flu.
Dose0.5 mL for adults and older children. For infants and toddlers, dose and minimum age depend on the product label.
RouteIntramuscular injection in the upper arm (deltoid). Thigh in infants. Never into a vein.
WhenBefore winter, ideally September to November. Protection builds in about 2 weeks. Later is still worthwhile.
How oftenEvery year, because flu strains change and protection fades.
Standard scheduleMost adults: 1 dose a year. Children under 9 having flu vaccine for the first time: 2 doses at least 4 weeks apart.
IRC high-risk advice2 doses of Anflu, one month apart, for maximum protection.

The second dose in adults goes beyond the standard international schedule, and good evidence for it is still limited. It is not harmful, and your doctor will decide whether it suits you.

Contraindications and precautions

  • Do not give if you had a severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) to a previous flu vaccine or to any ingredient.
  • Postpone if you have a fever or a moderate to severe illness. A mild cold is not a reason to delay.
  • Tell the doctor about egg allergy. Most people can still be vaccinated, with 15 to 30 minutes of observation.
  • Tell the doctor about Guillain-Barré syndrome, bleeding disorders or blood thinners. Pressure on the site for 2 minutes helps.
  • Pregnancy is not a contraindication. The vaccine is recommended.

Side effects

  • Common, 1 to 2 days: pain, redness or swelling at the injection site, mild fever, headache, muscle aches, tiredness. This is not flu.
  • Fainting can happen, especially in teenagers. Sit for 15 minutes afterwards.
  • Rare: serious allergy (hives, swollen face, wheeze, breathlessness), usually within minutes to hours. This is an emergency.
  • Very rare: Guillain-Barré syndrome, about 1 to 2 cases per million doses.

After the vaccine: follow-up

  • Stay at the clinic for 15 minutes after the injection.
  • For pain or fever, take paracetamol and move your arm. Do not use aspirin in children.
  • Protection starts after about 2 weeks. Until then, wash hands and avoid sick contacts.
  • If a second dose is advised, book it for 4 weeks later.
  • Repeat the vaccine every year. Adults 65 and over and people with lung disease should also ask about the pneumococcal vaccine, which prevents bacterial pneumonia.
  • If you still get flu symptoms (fever, cough, body aches), see a doctor early. Antiviral treatment works best within 48 hours, especially in high-risk patients.
  • Go to emergency for breathlessness, chest pain, blue lips, confusion or a fever lasting more than 3 days.

Common questions

Can the flu vaccine give me flu?

No. It contains killed virus. Mild aches for a day or two are the immune system responding.

If I am vaccinated, can I still get flu?

Yes, but usually a milder illness with fewer complications. The vaccine also does not protect against the common cold or COVID-19.

Is it safe to take Vaxigrip or Anflu with other vaccines?

Usually yes, at a different injection site. Tell the doctor which vaccines you are due for.

انفلوئنزا (فلو) ویکسین: مقصد، خوراک، حفاظت اور فالو اپ

ڈاکٹر جہانگیر خان، کنسلٹنٹ پلمونولوجسٹ · نظرثانی: ڈاکٹر محمد اسرار · اپڈیٹ: 4 اکتوبر 2026

اس سیزن کے اہم نکات
  • اس سیزن انفلووک (Influvac) کی کمی ہے۔
  • IRC میں ہم ویکسی گرپ (Vaxigrip) یا اینفلو (Anflu) تجویز کرتے ہیں، جو دستیاب ہو۔
  • زیادہ خطرے والے مریضوں میں زیادہ سے زیادہ حفاظت کے لیے ڈاکٹر جہانگیر خان اینفلو کی دو خوراکیں، ایک مہینے کے وقفے سے تجویز کرتے ہیں۔
  • ویکسین کا مقصد فلو کی پیچیدگیوں، خاص طور پر نمونیا، سے بچانا اور اموات کم کرنا ہے۔

فلو ویکسین کیوں ضروری ہے؟

فلو صرف شدید نزلہ نہیں ہے۔ انفلوئنزا وائرس سانس کی نالیوں کو متاثر کرتا ہے اور وائرل نمونیا کر سکتا ہے، یا پھیپھڑوں کو کمزور کر کے بیکٹیریا والا دوسرا نمونیا ہونے دیتا ہے۔ یہ دمے اور COPD کے دورے بھی بھڑکاتا ہے، دل کی کمزوری اور ذیابیطس کو بگاڑتا ہے، اور انفیکشن کے بعد کے ہفتوں میں دل کے دورے کا خطرہ بڑھاتا ہے۔ پاکستان میں فلو سردیوں میں زیادہ ہوتا ہے۔ ویکسین ہر بار انفیکشن نہیں روکتی، لیکن ویکسین شدہ لوگوں کو فلو ہو بھی جائے تو نمونیا، ہسپتال میں داخلے اور موت کا امکان کم ہوتا ہے۔

ویکسین کا مقصد

  • فلو سے بچاؤ، یا بیماری ہلکی اور مختصر کرنا
  • نمونیا اور دوسری پیچیدگیوں سے بچاؤ
  • ہسپتال میں داخلے اور اموات میں کمی، خاص طور پر بزرگوں اور دائمی مریضوں میں
  • فلو سے ہونے والے دمے اور COPD کے دوروں سے بچاؤ
  • اپنے ارد گرد کے لوگوں، جیسے بچوں اور بزرگ رشتہ داروں، کی حفاظت
